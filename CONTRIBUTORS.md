# CERG Contributors

CERG is shaped by people who test ideas, ask hard questions, identify gaps, and improve the work. This register recognizes community participation in CERG.

Listings are opt-in and maintainer-approved. A listing recognizes participation in the CERG community; it does not imply organizational endorsement, certification, or responsibility for the framework. Contributors may request removal at any time.

## Claim your listing

If you received an invitation, edit **only your row**:

1. Add your preferred name, if you would like it displayed.
2. Replace the starter text with a short description of your contribution.
3. Change `Invited` to `Confirmed`.
4. Open a pull request titled `docs: claim contributor listing for @your-handle`.

Maintainers review every change before publishing it. To add a new contributor listing, open an issue or contact a maintainer first so the contribution can be verified and the initial row can be prepared.
